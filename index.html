Compare the Policy

A plain-language look at what Australia's 34 federally registered political parties are proposing, brought together in one place so it's easier to compare.

Policy titles are exactly as published by each party. Categories are assigned by Compare the Policy to group similar policies together, and summaries are written from each party's own source material — both are marked with an ⓘ on every policy. First-pass research dataset prepared 21 September 2026; some sources are still being verified.

  1. 1 Every party is shown by default — filter down to just the ones you want to compare.
  2. 2 Policies are grouped into categories, with a plain-language summary underneath each one.
  3. 3 Check the source and the ⓘ note on any policy for where it came from.

Compare the Policy doesn't rank, score or recommend any party. It exists to make policies easier to find and understand, so you can make up your own mind.
